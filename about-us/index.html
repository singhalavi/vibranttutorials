About us

Online GCSE group tuition for Years 9–11.

We teach Maths, Physics and Chemistry, following the AQA and Pearson Edexcel specifications.

Meet the tutors

Our tutors are two Tonbridge-based parents and professionals: a Software Engineer and IIT Delhi graduate with a passion for Maths and Physics, and an Enterprise Architect with an Engineering degree and a strong grounding in Science. With children of their own preparing for GCSEs, they know what students need: clear explanations, solid fundamentals, and the confidence to tackle challenging exam questions.

How we teach

We run live, tutor-led, one-hour online group sessions. We break difficult topics into manageable steps, build secure foundations and help students practise applying ideas to GCSE-style questions. Our aim is to develop understanding, problem-solving skills and confidence.
